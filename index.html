About Me

Curious about silicon, circuits, and computation.

Hello! I am Priyanshu, a first-year undergraduate student pursuing a B.Tech in Electronics and Communication Engineering (ECE) with a focused specialization in Semiconductor and Chip Design at JECRC University in Jaipur, Rajasthan.

My journey into electronics begins with a simple question: how do atomic-scale silicon transistors coordinate to execute software, automate machines, and process signals? Rather than just using technology as a black box, I want to understand how electronic circuits, processors, integrated circuits, and embedded systems function from the ground up.

At my current stage, I have introductory familiarity with prototyping platforms like Arduino, alongside early hands-on exploration of the ESP32, Raspberry Pi, and general microcontroller-based setups. I am actively working on my programming foundations, problem-solving skills, and laboratory practices.

Looking forward across my degree, my objective is to systematically build deep foundations in digital electronics, semiconductor physics, computer architecture, and VLSI design.
